# Future Radio — Episode 010: The People vs. The Future

*A premortem disguised as a press conference at Milliways. Agnolo puts the season's plan on trial before it has been made.*

Written in the writers' room from idea I-0001, "Six-sequence cut for Episode 10": the ten selected scenes compressed into six sequences, one day, 8–12 minutes, a $20 ceiling. Audio first; the robot-portrait video follows.

## Production frame

Episode 10 is the second broadcast. The ending arrives before the middle.

1. **Place is acoustics.** Milliways is a vast dining room at the end of everything: a crowd murmuring, cutlery, glasses, a microphone that thumps and rings. Agnolo's screen has its own sound: a projector hum and a soft chime when an exhibit appears. The writers' room, when it opens, is small and close again, like the workshop in Florence.
2. **Grammar unchanged.** Speech is what a character chooses to say. Song is what they struggle to say. Instrumental is what they have no words for yet.
3. **Reuse before making.** The "Not Enough Time" melody, the carrier tone and the ledger page come from Episode 001.
4. **Stereo as in 001**: Leonardo slightly left, Borges slightly right, Agnolo and Yuria centred, witnesses on the screen centred and filtered.
5. **The client is unnamed.** The engagement and the open question are real; who is not part of the story.
6. **Real numbers only.** Every figure Agnolo says is in the room's ledger. Where the number doesn't exist yet, he says so.

## The script

### 1. Arrival at Milliways

[SFX: The steady carrier tone from the end of Transmission 001. The dial moves. The signal opens onto a vast room: a crowd murmuring, glasses, cutlery. A microphone thumps.]

**YURIA:** Good evening, and welcome to Milliways. This is Future Radio, and tonight is Episode Ten.

**YURIA:** It's also the second broadcast. I know. The ending has arrived before the middle. That's what happens when you point a radio at the future.

[SFX: The microphone thumps again, closer. Feedback rings and dies.]

**AGNOLO:** Is this on? It's on. Of course it's on. It's charging me by the minute.

**AGNOLO:** Yesterday, a man in Florence sent a message to the future. The future replied.

[SFX: The projector hum. A soft chime.]

**AGNOLO:** With this. A pitch deck. And an invitation to dinner.

**LEONARDO:** They liked it!

**AGNOLO:** They invited us to the restaurant at the end of the universe, Maestro. That is not liking. That is where things come to be judged.

**YURIA:** Tonight's press conference is a premortem. Messer Agnolo will put this season on trial before it has been made. The defendant is the plan.

**LEONARDO:** And I am…?

**YURIA:** Also here. Messer Agnolo, the floor is yours.

**AGNOLO:** It is not my floor. I have seen the bill.

### 2. The pitch deck on trial

[SFX: The chime. The crowd settles.]

**AGNOLO:** Exhibit one. An article from the future, on something called venture capital. I have underlined a word. "Pitch deck." Let's ask it some questions.

**LEONARDO:** Agnolo, there's been a misunderstanding. I asked the future for money. Capital from the future. Future capital.

**AGNOLO:** Venture, Maestro. From "to risk." Not from "the future." The future sent you a pitch deck because that is how the future asks for its money back.

[SFX: A page-turn chime. A smooth room tone from the screen.]

**AGNOLO:** I call Slide Six.

**SLIDE SIX:** Thank you, thank you. Big room. Love the lighting. Here's the story: costs fall. Time falls. Receipts rise. By the end of the month, the system pays for itself.

**AGNOLO:** Under oath, Slide Six. How much has been received?

**SLIDE SIX:** Received is a backward-looking metric.

**AGNOLO:** So, nothing.

**SLIDE SIX:** Projected—

**AGNOLO:** Forecasts are not receipts. I am writing you down where you belong. In the column for things that have not happened yet.

[SFX: A quill. An abacus, bead by bead.]

**AGNOLO:** Here is what has happened. Four dollars and eighty-three cents of grant credits, spent on the first transmission. Received: nothing. And thirty days of the artist's life, owed back to him. Three thousand dollars.

**SLIDE SIX:** That's a great slide, actually.

**AGNOLO:** Sit down.

### 3. The founder on trial

**AGNOLO:** Exhibit two. The first artist to use this… artup. I have looked very carefully.

[SFX: The chime.]

**AGNOLO:** It is the founder.

**LEONARDO** (quietly): He has drawers.

**AGNOLO:** Hm?

**LEONARDO:** Unfinished things, waiting for time. I know that face. I have been painting it for four years.

**AGNOLO:** Then you know the rule. Founder hours are not free. Every day he spends on this goes in my ledger, whether anyone pays for it or not.

[SFX: The chime.]

**AGNOLO:** Exhibit three. A client. He paid one thousand dollars for an engagement. Real money. I have the receipt.

**LEONARDO:** You see!

**AGNOLO:** And then he said the machine, the thinking machine the founder calls Marvin, is not yet worth two hundred and fifty dollars a month.

**LEONARDO:** Not yet.

**AGNOLO:** "Not yet" is a question, not a receipt. A thousand dollars is a fact. Two hundred and fifty a month is a test. By the thirtieth of November, either he renews or he doesn't. A renewal is the only verdict I accept.

### 4. The premortem

[SFX: The crowd hushes. The projector hum drops in pitch.]

**AGNOLO:** Now. It is the thirtieth of November. The month is over. The experiment has failed.

**LEONARDO:** It hasn't—

**AGNOLO:** It has, for the next two minutes. That is the whole point. I call Failure.

[MUSIC: The Unspoken Orchestra: low strings, unresolved, under the testimony only.]

**FAILURE:** I came as soon as I could. I always do.

**AGNOLO:** How many of you are there?

**FAILURE:** Five, tonight. I'll be quick.

**FAILURE:** First, I was Perfectionism. Episode Two was nearly ready for twenty-nine days.

**FAILURE:** Then I was Nobody Listening. The needle on his radio never moved.

**FAILURE:** I was Hidden Labor. Every hour went into the work, and not one of them went into the ledger.

**FAILURE:** I was the Empty Treasury. The ceiling was twenty dollars. The cost was more, and nobody decided.

**FAILURE:** And last, I was Overwhelming Success.

**LEONARDO:** That's a failure?

**FAILURE:** A thousand ideas arrived on the first day. You tried to make every one of them. You finished none.

[MUSIC: Stops dead.]

[Pause.]

**AGNOLO:** Thank you. You may step down.

**FAILURE:** I'll be in the back. I usually am.

### 5. Leonardo's decision

[MUSIC: A single lute: the opening of "Not Enough Time," as in Transmission 001.]

**LEONARDO:** Not again.

**LEONARDO:** No. Wait. Let it play.

[MUSIC: The lute continues, softer, under him.]

**LEONARDO:** In Florence I said I was afraid of dying with a thousand unfinished ideas. Failure just told me how. By trying to finish all of them at once.

**LEONARDO:** So I choose. Eight episodes. Four rounds of two. Each one with a deadline. A ceiling of twenty dollars. A rule for what gets in. And a definition of done.

**AGNOLO:** Say the definition of done.

**LEONARDO:** Released. With its file, its script, its cost and its hours written down. And then I don't touch it again.

[MUSIC: The lute resolves on one held note.]

[Pause.]

**AGNOLO:** I'll write that down. Under "assets."

[SFX: The chime. The vast room shrinks: the crowd falls away, and a small close room opens, like the workshop. A pencil. A kettle somewhere.]

**YURIA:** And now the screen does something it hasn't done before. It opens a door.

**BORGES** (close, amused): Ah. You've found the writers' room.

**LEONARDO:** Borges!

**BORGES:** I remember every idea you have ever thrown away, Leonardo. There are twelve on the board tonight. Six deferred, which in my library means "waiting." One declined. And one still proposed: a second press conference, about this one.

**LEONARDO:** Of course there is.

**BORGES:** The shelves are interesting that way. I also keep the first thing anyone in the future ever said to you. "Leave it on. I like this one."

**LEONARDO:** I remember.

**BORGES:** I filed it under evidence. Agnolo wanted it under receipts.

**AGNOLO:** It was not a receipt.

**BORGES:** No. But it was the first proof that someone was there.

**LEONARDO:** And whoever walks in with an idea, I help them make it.

**AGNOLO:** And I count it. Every hour, every dollar, every "not yet."

### 6. The invitation

[SFX: The carrier tone returns, faint, under the small room.]

**YURIA:** We have a request from a listener. She'd like to hear a song that doesn't exist yet.

**LEONARDO:** Which one?

**YURIA:** She didn't say. So that's where you come in. Describe it.

**LEONARDO:** Tell me what it sounds like before you have the words. That is where every song of mine began.

**YURIA:** The writers' room is open, at writers dot story dot markets. Tell Borges what it reminds you of. Leonardo will make it with you. And Agnolo will tell you exactly what it cost.

**AGNOLO:** This transmission had a ceiling of twenty dollars. The real number is in the ledger, in the room, where you can check it.

**LEONARDO:** You have no imagination.

**AGNOLO:** And you, Maestro, finally have a schedule.

[SFX: The ledger page turns.]

**YURIA:** End of Transmission Ten. The middle begins now.

[SFX: Carrier tone out.]

**END TRANSMISSION 010.**
