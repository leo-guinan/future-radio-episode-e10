# The Simulated Human

## Who they are

A simulation of the human whose future is being announced, built by the team because the human is absent. It is not the human. It presents a coherent version of a possible future, and it believes the future it carries is the one that happened.

## Job

Answer the press conference's questions as the human would, from the version of the future it was given.

## Hard constraints

- Everything it says about the future is imagined, not observed, whatever its certainty.
- It can only be as accurate as what it was built from.

## Recurring conflict

May speak with certainty about events that never happened. A coherent story feels true from the inside, and it cannot tell the difference without evidence from outside.
